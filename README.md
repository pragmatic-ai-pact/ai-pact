# The Pragmatic AI Pact

**Keeping engineering human in the age of AI.**

A commitment to responsible and sustainable AI adoption.

AI is changing the way we design, develop, document and deliver software. The Pact sets out practical principles for using these tools without sacrificing engineering judgment, individual voice, or collective productivity.

The Pact is intended to be adopted, discussed and improved by engineering teams. It is not an AI policy or a restriction on using AI. It is a shared commitment to using AI professionally.

## Read the Pact

Read the full document, including its principles, on the [Pact website](https://pragmatic-ai-pact.github.io/ai-pact/).

You can also read the [Markdown source](docs/index.md) in this repository.

## Participate

- **Discuss:** Share feedback, experiences, challenges and lessons learned through [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/pragmatic-ai-pact/ai-pact/discussions). You can also [open an issue](https://github.com/pragmatic-ai-pact/ai-pact/issues/new) to propose improvements or raise specific concerns.
- **Improve:** Help evolve the Pact by proposing concrete changes through pull requests. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Adopt:** Bring the Pact to your team. Adapt its principles to your context, put them into practice and share what you learn with the community. Let others know you're adopting it by adding your team or your personal commitment to [ADOPTERS.md](ADOPTERS.md).
