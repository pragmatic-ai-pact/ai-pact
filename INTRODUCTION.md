**A commitment to responsible and sustainable AI adoption**

<!-- introduction-body -->

AI should help us become better engineers, not just generate more content.
A 4,000-line PR may take minutes to produce, but it still takes time to review, validate, and maintain.
When that work is pushed onto teammates, productivity gains become review burden and AI Brain Fry.
This Pact is not about restricting AI. It is about using it professionally and sharing its benefits.
