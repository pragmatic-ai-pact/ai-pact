# Code of Conduct

The Pragmatic AI Pact community is committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

We expect participants to:
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and proposals, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Keep discussions relevant and useful to the community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community constructive.
